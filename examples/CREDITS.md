## House of 40 Knots

What the study takes from the published drawings and photos: two street façades meeting at a corner; one brick
band per storey whose top edge rises and falls like a hill, so the screen is also the window railing; bricks
threaded on vertical bars; open knots in front of the glass, filler and raised knots in front of walls and floor
edges; each band set out at a slightly different angle; and the way the façade was built, as rows of
"filler, hollow, raised" read aloud to the bricklayers the way a carpet pattern is read to weavers.

What is estimated by eye: every dimension, the hill profiles, the window bays, how far each band stands out, and
the place of each single raised brick. The building behind the screens is not modelled.

The Knot Pattern component and the Knots option of Rod Screen were written after this project.

Sources: project drawings and photographs as published by ArchLine (archline.ir), the Aga Khan Award for
Architecture project page, ArchDaily, Archinect.
