# Food4Rhino page (draft)

**Name:** Hezarbaaf · هزارباف
**Short description:** Buildable parametric brick walls for Grasshopper, with Persian brickwork built in.

**Description**
Hezarbaaf turns curves, lofts, sections and surfaces into real brick walls, brick by brick, in real formats
and bonds, and keeps them buildable: every turned, projecting or removed brick is checked for support, overlap
and corbel limits, and shown in red when a builder would have a problem.

- Walls from any curve, polylines with interlocking corners, lofts, sections, any surface, round towers
- Openings typed or drawn in Rhino; semicircular, segmental, pointed, soldier and Persian panj-o-haft arches
- Design with fields (attractor, wave, ripple, noise, image) and operations (rotate, protrude, remove, perforate)
- Brick tones, glaze, glass bricks, mortar joints; rod screens and knot patterns
- Persian identity: Hezarbaaf woven relief, square Kufic, the Persian square brick, a full Persian interface
- To site: quantities and cost, Raj sheets course by course, 1:1 rotation stencils, elevation drawings,
  assembly order for robots, bake as blocks; Ladybug link for sun-driven walls
